About Us

Who We Are

Manav Seva Trust

Manav Seva Trust is a registered public charitable trust based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. At our Bal Ashram, on 10 Km. Indore-Ujjain Road at Gram Revti, children who need care find a safe, secured and serene home where they can grow physically, mentally and spiritually.

Along with regular education, every child is given nutritious food, a place to live, and the chance to take part in sports, drama, gardening and daily prayers.

Our Work

Education with Values

The Trust also runs Devi Ahilya Ved Vidyalaya, established with the cooperation of Maharshi Ved Vyas Pratishthan, Pune. Here Vedic students study the Vedas along with Sanskrit, English and computers, while residence, food, clothing and medical care are provided free of cost.

Our Campus

A Home for All Round Development

Spread over 7 acres of cultivated land with two tube wells, the campus has 18 rooms that accommodate four children each, a common hall, dining hall, kitchen, a Dhyan Kendra for meditation and prayer, a play ground and a computer room. Every child has a separate bed, cupboard and furniture.

Registration

A Registered Public Trust

Manav Seva Trust is registered as a Public Trust under Section 6 of the M.P. Public Trust Act, 1951. Contributions to the Trust are eligible for 50% exemption under Section 80-G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and the Trust is registered under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act.
