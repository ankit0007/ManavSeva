Our Children

We Help Them Grow

Physically, Mentally & Spiritually

Besides education, our children take part in other meaningful activities like indoor and outdoor sports, drama and gardening.

We involve them in morning and evening prayers for inculcating spiritual values.

We provide them a balanced and nutritious diet.

Our Campus

Infrastructure & Facilities

  • 7 acre cultivated land with two tube wells
  • Safe, secured and serene environment for all round development
  • 18 rooms (each room accommodating four members), common hall, dining hall, kitchen etc.
  • Dhyan Kendra (Meditation & Prayer Hall)
  • Play ground
  • Computer room
  • Separate bed, cupboard and furniture for each individual
